HOW I SOLVE PROBLEMS

Start with the constraint.
Design the workflow around it.

I make business rules explicit in the data model, access checks and service flow, then connect the interface and external services around that foundation.

Laravel & PHPREST APIsSQL data modelingVue & InertiaQueues & integrations

01 · TENANT BOUNDARIES

Keep each institution’s work separate

Problem: one learning platform serves multiple institutions, each with its own users, roles and learning activity.

Solution: model institution-scoped workflows and enforce permissions at the backend while supporting branded tenant access.

Applied across Byaan and ELLI · Laravel · MySQL

02 · CAPACITY-AWARE BOOKING

Offer only bookable service slots

Problem: a booking depends on service location, time, available crew and operational capacity—not just an open calendar cell.

Solution: connect service areas, crew availability and slot capacity to booking, pricing, payment and customer notification flows.

Glitz · Laravel · MySQL · REST APIs

03 · SLOW WORK & CONNECTED DATA

Keep long-running processing out of the request

Problem: AI and document tasks depend on product knowledge and data held across enterprise inventory systems.

Solution: ground answers in product information, connect Oracle and MySQL workflows, and move document and AI processing to queued jobs.

Shoura Online · Laravel · Oracle · MySQL · Queues
